Privacy Policy

Pureism Hop Loop
Last updated: 17 November 2026

Overview

Pureism Hop Loop is an offline arcade game designed to run entirely on your device. The game does not require an account or an Internet connection.

Information collection and sharing

Pureism Hop Loop does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or other media, advertising data, diagnostics, or gameplay analytics.

The game contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, social-login, billing, or Play Games integration and does not offer in-app purchases.

Local game information

Score and coin values are held temporarily in memory while you play. They are not saved to persistent storage. Closing the app clears this temporary game state.

Accounts, retention, and deletion

Pureism Hop Loop has no account system and stores no personal information. As a result, there is no account data or stored personal information for the game to retain or delete.

Children's privacy

Pureism Hop Loop does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security and third parties

The game performs no network communication. Google Play and your device's operating system may process information independently under their own privacy terms; this policy describes the data practices of Pureism Hop Loop itself.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.
